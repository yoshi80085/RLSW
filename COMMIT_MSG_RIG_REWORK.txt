rig rework: off the skill tree, onto the marquee and the stacks

The rig stops being something you buy in round three and becomes something you
play into existence and then have to maintain. Three axes, three clocks, no Db.

🫁 THE RADIUS BREATHES (SEQUENCING.md §5.A⁷)
    radius = RIG_RADIUS_FLOOR + (your turn ? Drive : Sustain).length
The turn split is not decoration — every offensive read of `inRange` already
happened on your own turn and every defensive one on somebody else's, so each
gate is handed the stack it was already about. Floor 3 + the root-alone seed puts
every Spirit at 4 on turn one, exactly the old tier-0 radius, so the resting
board is unchanged. RIG_RADIUS_BY_TIER is deleted, not deprecated; there is no
infinite radius left. `sonicRig` takes the note state and an `onTurn` flag that
defaults to the DEFENSIVE reading, so a forgotten argument under-reaches rather
than inventing reach. `evaluate.js:898`, logged as drift for a session, goes
through `rigFor` now and can finally see a blown amp.

🎪 THE MARQUEE IS A CHOICE CARD (MARQUEE_QUIZ_DESIGN.md §2, §4-§5)
Lane × difficulty, picked face-down before the question is drawn — that ordering
is the whole skill component. CROWD pays fans; RIG pays 1/2/3 tiers spent at the
card on pool or power. Ceiling is 3+3, exactly Amp III / Power III, so §2.5's
"max Sonic roll is 8" needed no re-checking. Atrophy sheds one tier per 3 of the
owner's own turns without training, power before pool (shedding pool first would
silently drop both), floored at the old free grant — total neglect lands where
everyone starts. 20 new RIG-lane questions; buckets now ≥22 each, asserted.

🐛 AND THE QUIZ WAS CLIENT-ONLY, WHICH WOULD HAVE FROZEN EVERY BENCH SPIRIT
A headless Spirit walked onto a marquee and nothing happened — not declared in
HARNESS_GAPS, absent. Survivable while the rig was a purchase; fatal once the
marquee is the only source of pool and power. `collectPickups` resolves it now,
which meant moving `fansFromDeed` into the engine (the fan-gain rule was a React
function, so the bench could not pay a single fan), moving `usedTrivia` out of a
ref into engine state, and making the draw pure with PER-BUCKET recycling.

🔊 THE EVALUATOR HAD NO OPINION ABOUT VOLUME, AND IT INVERTED (§5.D⁷)
Bots sat 1 hex from a live marquee on 54 decision points of a 43-turn match and
stepped on it once: taking a marquee consumes it, so proximity value FALLS when
you collect, and nothing rose to meet it. Two rows fix it — `marqueeSeek` pays
the walk (scaled by headroom), `loud` pays for having gone. ⚠️ The sweep found
the pathology is in the seek row: at 1.5 match length triples and FEWER Spirits
end up with a rig. Raise `loud`, never `marqueeSeek`.

🪓 AND THE BRANCH IS DELETED (§5.G⁷)
The whole `electric` route — amps, power, range, Overcharge. 110 Db, the largest
sink in the game. Every reader cleaned: bot skill orders, STARTING_SKILLS, the
`amp_1` seed, the Rock God scoring, the client's unlock logs, and two duplicate
copies of the pool table that had drifted. Overcharge was CUT rather than
rehoused — with no amp gate left, a free 12 Db upgrade on turn two is a different
skill from the one designed.

🪦 Two gates that could never fire went with it. ULTIMATE_PREREQS named three ids
that are not in the tree; no skill carried `prereq: '__all_pa__'`. Both green in
selftest against a fake tree written to match the gate rather than the game —
CLAUDE.md §15, verbatim, for the second time. Replaced by the assertion that
would have caught it: every prereq in the real tree must name a skill that exists.

⚠️ One live trap, pinned rather than fixed: most Spirits now start with an EMPTY
unlockedSkills, so the old "is the list empty" grant gate would APPEAR to work —
except for the Ronin, who holds theory_minor. Asymmetric, and one broken
character is far harder to spot than four. b0check asserts the disagreement.
