What should happen — the beats

  1. Shove. The game's own skate, two hexes, ends on the mortar.
  2. Contact. The trip-plate gives (click), the piece sinks a touch; the barrel flares white.
  3. Fuse + hit-stop. A beat of dread, then the world freezes on the flash frame and drops into slow motion.
  4. Blast. Petals fold back, a flame column punches up, the shell goes on to burst overhead, and the piece is thrown through it, tumbling.
  5. Land. Squash, dust, a dull thud and the acrylic rattling; a couple of dying bounces.
  6. After. Dazed sway (or flat, or flat-then-up). Print scorched, flames licking for the Burn.
