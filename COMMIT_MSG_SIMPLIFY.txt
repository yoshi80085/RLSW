Cut the Db economy from nine sources to four

A review against the project's own three goals found chords/melody in good
shape, Theory at risk, and the stated premise - SIMPLIFY - failing outright:
PENDING_CHANGES had gone 609 -> 1400 lines implementing a plan whose thesis was
"eight mechanics -> one", and a single commit moved the Db number nine ways.

New: src/engine/dbaudit.mjs, a harness mirroring confirmNoteTrack that scores
3000 commits per Theory tier. It changed every decision here. Findings:

- Harmonic Lock paid NOTHING for a major or minor triad (rank 4 was banded
  out). The one mechanic built to reward chord building ignored the only chord
  a new player can build - the stack cap is 3 until Blues/Dom7. It measured a
  flat 0.00 across the first three Theory tiers.
- Theory's first two rungs were economically invisible: income by tier ran
  3.25 / 3.31 / 3.30 / 4.83 / 5.20. Eighteen Db moved income by -0.01.
- The capstone's headline chromatic payout fired on 1% of commits, worth 0.02
  Db each - 16 Db of ladder buying something nobody would see. Every one of its
  assertions passed. Tests prove a mechanic works, not that it matters.

Db now pays for FACTS, not taste:

  earned = max(0, length + ending + lock - penalty)

Cut: the Style system entirely (its detectors re-scored gestures the Drive and
Sustain boosts already pay for - detectRepeatPattern was literally the same
function the Sustain boost calls); the Drive/Sustain overflow into Db (13% of
income, paid out of the half of a comparison that lost); the chromatic payout.

The Performance Score now feeds the crowd and only the crowd. It is the game's
aesthetic judge, that question has no honest answer, and that makes it a bad Db
source and a good crowd source - a crowd's taste is supposed to be
impressionistic. It keeps driving excitement, loyalty, fans and Fame. This also
answers unresolved item 3: Theory owns Db, showmanship owns Fame.

Fixed: Harmonic Lock pays triads (rank 4-5 = 1, 6-7 = 2, 8 = 3).

theory_chromatic now grants stack slot 6 - slots are what make the ladder pay.
That fix did nothing on its own and the audit caught it: the largest chord
template was 5 notes, so a 6-note stack evaluated as a plain dom9. Added
Dominant 13 and Minor 11 at rank 8, pinned by an assertion that the biggest
chord must exactly fill the biggest stack.

DB_UPGRADE_THRESHOLD 6 -> 4: mean Db 3.98 -> 2.57.

Income by tier, before and after:
  before  3.25  3.31  3.30  4.83  5.20   (flat for three rungs)
  after   1.96  2.20  2.41  3.02  3.24   (climbs at every rung)

Cutting the noise un-masked the Theory ladder - the flat sources were drowning
the tier signal, so one change served both goals.

Also fixed: a seeded-LCG fuzz drew with `seed % n`, and LCG low bits are
degenerate - rnd(2) returned 0 on 99.7% of draws, so C1's fuzz generated almost
only descending runs while reporting 4000 cases of coverage. Take the high bits.

Task C is retired in full. b0check: 60 -> 53 groups, all passing.

Not verified in the Linux sandbox: vite build (pre-existing bus error) and
eslint on the 13.9k-line simulator. Both need a run on Windows.
