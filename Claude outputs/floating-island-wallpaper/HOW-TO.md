# Floating Island wallpaper

## PC: Lively Wallpaper

### Install, or update an old version
1. In Lively, if an older **Floating Island** tile is there, click its **⋯** and choose **Delete**.
2. Click **+ Add Wallpaper → Browse** and pick `Floating-Island-Lively.zip`.
3. Click the **Floating Island** tile to set it.

### What's on screen
- **Clock (top-left):** time, date, today's weather for Yamagata, and the month calendar. Japanese holidays are dotted pink; hover one to see its name.
- **Fan (opposite corner):** hover over it for today's weather; click it for a headline, and click again for the next one.
- **Sky:** follows Yamagata's real sunrise and sunset:
  - Night (violet)
  - Dawn (dark twilight blue)
  - Day (blue)
  - Dusk (dark pink-purple)
- **Sun:** on clear days it crosses the sky, then fades into a rose afterglow at sunset.
- **On the hour:** lightning, a cheer from the crowd, and the fan calls the time.
- **Music:** when music plays on your PC, the spotlights, crowd and fan move to the beat.

### Using it
On an empty part of the desktop:

| Do this | What happens |
|---|---|
| Click | Lightning |
| Drag | Turn the island |
| Scroll | Zoom |

This needs **Lively Settings → Wallpaper → Wallpaper input → Mouse**, which is on by default.

### Settings
Click the tile's **⋯** and choose **Customise**. You can change:
- **City:** for weather and sunrise times.
- **Display:** °C or °F, 12- or 24-hour clock, and the clock corner (the fan always takes the opposite one).
- **What shows:** each panel can be turned on or off.
- **The fan:** its look and colour.
- **News source:** world news or tech news.
- **Music:** the reaction and how sensitive it is.
- **Sky:** daytime nebula strength, glow and lightning.
- **Motion and framing:** turn speed, island size and height.
- **Performance:** 30 or 60 fps.

If the PC feels slow, choose **30 fps**, or **Normal** resolution.

`preview.html` is the same wallpaper in a browser, with test buttons for each sky, the sun, music and the chime.

### Hotkeys
The `hotkeys` folder adds two keyboard shortcuts:

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Ctrl + Alt + W** | Turns the wallpaper off and on. Off closes it completely, freeing all its memory, and Windows' normal wallpaper shows. On loads it again, which takes a few seconds. |
| **Ctrl + Alt + Shift + W** | Pauses and resumes it. This is instant, but it stays in memory. |

To install them:
1. Double-click **Install hotkeys.cmd** with Lively running. It checks that it can find Lively and the wallpaper, and says "All good" if it can.
2. If a key does nothing the first time, sign out of Windows and back in once.

To remove them, double-click **Uninstall hotkeys.cmd**. To change the keys, open Start → Floating Island, right-click a shortcut → **Properties** → **Shortcut key**. If something goes wrong, the log is `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FloatingIsland\hotkey.log`.

## iPhone: Spatial Scene (iOS 26)
1. Get one of the `iphone-*.png` images onto your phone and save it to Photos. Email it to yourself, or open this chat in the Claude iPhone app.
   - `iphone-night.png`
   - `iphone-dawn.png`
   - `iphone-day.png`
   - `iphone-dusk.png`
2. Go to **Settings → Wallpaper → + Add New Wallpaper → Photos** and pick it. Don't zoom in.
3. Tap the **Spatial Scene** button (bottom-right), then **Add**.
4. If it doesn't move, check two things:
   - **Low Power Mode** is off.
   - **Settings → Apps → Photos** has spatial photo creation turned on. It needs iPhone 12 or later.
