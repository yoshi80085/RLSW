What's blocking what

two chains · one shared stall

Almost every open thread runs through one of two chains. The top chain is stalled on a character who was parked as a side project — and quietly became load-bearing. One branch of it came free on 4 Sep and was built the same day; its client half is now a preview page sitting in your browser.

Metalness is still the stall. His four abilities have no cooldowns at all — so the Ronin's new Shamisen, which steals cooldown off a rival, would do literally nothing against him. A redesign that was a parallel side project is sitting across the Ronin's path. If the Shamisen is wanted before Metalness, that's a trade worth making on purpose rather than discovering halfway through.

And the built branch is now a port away, not a decision away. Shukuchi runs in the engine and has its own suite; the button, the arc and the landing overlay were previewed and checked against the real engine at 896 assertions, so the picture cannot quietly disagree with the game — and you dialled it in the same day. Your settings live in the Ronin doc, not in the preview, which keeps no state. Until the port lands, the search bot still plans hops a human can't take.

The Ronin ledger

open decision · nobody's answered it yet

Four consecutive passes have made this character weaker, and each one deferred the compensation to the next. No single change looks unreasonable, which is exactly the problem — so it's written down here rather than deferred a fifth time.

Four subtractions, one addition Filed 4 Sep in CHARACTER_HANDOFF.md. Not a rebalance proposal — balance is parked on purpose. This is a slot question.
  1. 2 Sep The Chord Tone Pardon he was born with became the roster's floor when the Theory ladder went universal. He lost a head start; nobody else gave anything up.
  2. 3 Sep The drift sweep found his card text still selling that head start as his. Corrected — honest text, no compensation.
  3. 4 Sep Wa no Koe (12 Db) cut. Meanwhile Bushido 6→8 and Illusion 6→10 got dearer, and Bushido, Illusion and the Shamisen all got slower.
  4. 4 Sep The deletion landed. The stated consolation for #1 was "Wa no Koe still stacks on top exactly as designed" — that sentence is now void.
  5. 4 Sep 🌀 Shukuchi built, so he's back to four abilities. This does not close the ledger: it's a 6 Db mobility tool where a 12 Db payout used to be, it pays no Drive, no Sustain and no Fame, and the 12 Db slot is still empty.
The question, in one line: the Ronin is now a four-ability Spirit whose kit costs more and cools slower than it did a week ago, with his most expensive rung removed — is that the intended shape of a burst character, or is he owed a slot?
