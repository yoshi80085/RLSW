CAMERA + MOVE TILES

Camera: a move is now a slow push-in on the Spirit that keeps the camera's angle, like a Ken Burns effect, instead of a swing to the side. Between actions it stays on the acting Spirit and its surroundings. The wide shot only comes in after nothing has happened for a while (60 s, your setting).

Move tiles: the hexes you can step to glow with a magenta outline and a faint fill (your dial-in). The ring of pips at the Spirit's feet is the steps you have left; it empties as you walk. Click a lit tile to step there. Use Tiles: TODAY'S to compare with what the game draws now.
